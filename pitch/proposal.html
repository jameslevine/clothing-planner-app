Overview

The opportunity

We propose to design and build a private, two-part product: an elegant iPhone application for you, and a secure web studio for your wardrobe staff. Together they turn a large personal collection into something effortless to search, plan, wear and care for, with privacy and discretion built into every layer.

A focused dashboard for your team to upload, tag, plan, prepare and track, never seen by anyone outside your household. A few representative screens:

Privacy & Compliance

Your privacy, in plain terms

This app is built for you alone, and keeping your information private is the starting point, not an afterthought. In short: your data stays in Europe, only people you invite can see it, you can have it all deleted whenever you ask, and the studio can sign a confidentiality agreement. Below is what that means, without the jargon.

Where your information is kept

  • Stored on secure, well-known cloud services based in Europe (Ireland & Germany)
  • It never leaves the region you choose, and we can put that in writing
  • Everything is scrambled (encrypted) so that even if a file were intercepted, it can't be read
  • Safe backup copies are kept in case of any technical problem

Who can see it

  • Access is invitation-only: there is no public sign-up, and strangers cannot find or join
  • Each staff member only sees what their role needs
  • The app keeps a simple record of who did what, and when
  • We can remove anyone's access instantly the moment you ask

It's your data, and you can delete it

  • Everything in the app belongs to you; we only ever handle it on your instruction
  • Ask, and we will permanently delete any or all of it, or hand you a full copy
  • You decide how long things are kept
  • We follow UK and EU privacy law, with a written agreement to back it up

Kept confidential

  • The studio can sign a confidentiality agreement (NDA)
  • We tell you exactly which outside services are involved, and you approve them
  • Access is invitation-only, with every action logged and revocable at any time
